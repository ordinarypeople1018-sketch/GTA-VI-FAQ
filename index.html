How answers are handled

We believe players deserve accurate information, not misleading promises.

✓

Verified information

Confirmed answers are based on official announcements or reliable sources. Each confirmed answer includes its last checked date.

◷

Pending answers

If reliable information is unavailable, the question stays pending. We don’t invent answers just to fill a page.

◎

Rumours stay rumours

Speculation and community rumours are never presented as confirmed facts. We clearly distinguish them from verified information.
